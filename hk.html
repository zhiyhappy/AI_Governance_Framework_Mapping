Sources & Notes

The page uses HKMA TM-G-1 as the primary regulatory source and the cited third-party page as a theme-navigation reference. The domain-style labels visible on the third-party page are now explicitly shown in the Domain Reference tab and in the overview panel. The labels Governance, Information Security, IT Operations and Cyber are treated as reference themes and reconciled with practical TM-G-1 core areas; TM-G-2, TM-E-1 and OR-2 are shown as adjacent HKMA modules rather than counted as TM-G-1 core areas.
