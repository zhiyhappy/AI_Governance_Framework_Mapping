PQC & Quantum Readiness

A dedicated view of the post-quantum cryptography (PQC) direction for the Hong Kong banking sector, based on the HKMA paper “Quantum Preparedness of Hong Kong’s Banking Sector” (July 2026) — a strategic priority under Fintech 2030 to strengthen business, technology and quantum resilience. It presents the HKMA four-stage migration action plan, its strategic recommendations, and how each required capability maps to Palo Alto Networks solutions. A Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer (CRQC) would eventually break the classical public-key cryptography (RSA, ECC) protecting banking data, payments, authentication and financial market infrastructure, so the HKMA expects Authorized Institutions (AIs) to begin early, act in an orderly fashion, and build crypto-agility.

Primary source: HKMA, Quantum Preparedness of Hong Kong’s Banking Sector, July 2026 (survey of all AIs; analysis supported by KPMG). Quantum-risk expectations feed into the AI’s existing technology-risk framework under TM-G-1, alongside operational resilience and third-party risk. The HKMA’s stance is to encourage early, orderly preparation now rather than mandate a fixed migration deadline — while long-lived confidential data is already exposed to “Harvest Now, Decrypt Later” (HNDL) risk.

PQC × TM-G-1 Core Area — How It Fits

Quantum preparedness is not a new, eighth TM-G-1 core area — it is a layer that cuts across several existing ones. The HKMA is explicit that quantum risk should be embedded into the existing technology-risk (TM-G-1), operational-resilience and third-party-risk frameworks. The cards below map the PQC work back to this tool’s TM-G-1 core areas and control themes, so the standalone view stays connected to the main mapping.

PQC Sources & Notes

Primary: HKMA, Quantum Preparedness of Hong Kong’s Banking Sector, July 2026 (all-AI survey; four-stage action plan; Quantum Preparedness Index; six strategic recommendations). Aligned to Fintech 2030.
Standards: NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA); NIST IR 8547 Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards.
International guidance cited: BIS Quantum-readiness for the financial system: a roadmap (2025); PQC Coalition Migration Roadmap (2025); UK NCSC PQC migration timelines (2025); Australian Signals Directorate Planning for PQC (2025); EU Coordinated Implementation Roadmap (2025).
PANW lens: Strata — ZTNA / SASE / NGFW (quantum-safe / hybrid VPN, crypto visibility), Strata — Certificate Lifecycle Management (certificate/PKI lifecycle, discovery & crypto-agility), Strata — Centralized Visibility & OAM, Strata — Enterprise Browser, Strata — AI Security (Use/Build/Run), Cortex — CNAPP Cloud Security, Cortex — Autonomous SOC, Cortex — XDR / XSOAR / Xpanse, Idira — Identity Security (PAM / EPM / IAM / IGA / Secret Management / Agentic Identities).
Key terms: CRQC = Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer; HNDL = Harvest Now, Decrypt Later; CBOM = Cryptographic Bill of Materials (cryptographic inventory).
